OUR STORY

Local roots. Lasting results.

Citywide Real Estate & Property Management was founded on a simple belief: property owners and tenants deserve a partner who is transparent, responsive, and genuinely invested in their success.

Headquartered in Kennesaw, Georgia, we serve property owners, investors, buyers, and renters across the greater Atlanta metro. Whether you own a single rental home or a growing investment portfolio, we bring the same level of care and professionalism to every client.

We combine deep local market knowledge with modern tools and systems — so you get the personal attention of a boutique firm with the reliability and reach of a full-service brokerage.
